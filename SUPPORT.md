## Questions & bugs

- Usage questions and bug reports: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/jooservices/media-downloader/issues)
- Please include: browser and version, the platform (Instagram, Facebook or X), the URL kind (profile, post, reel), what you expected and what happened. Never paste cookies, tokens or private media.
